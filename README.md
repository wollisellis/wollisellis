# Ellis Wollis

**Ellis Wollis Malta Abhulime** · Nutritionist and AI researcher

Visiting Researcher at the [ARTORG Center for Biomedical Engineering Research](https://www.artorg.unibe.ch/) (AIHN Lab, University of Bern, Switzerland), through a FAPESP BEPE research internship abroad. MSc Researcher at PPG-CNEM (School of Applied Sciences, University of Campinas, LabMAS).

- Evaluation of generative AI in healthcare
- Biomedical knowledge graphs
- Multimodal food visual question answering
